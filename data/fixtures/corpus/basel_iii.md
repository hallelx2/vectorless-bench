# Acme Bank — Capital Adequacy Report

This report summarises Acme Bank's regulatory capital position under the Basel III
framework. Figures are presented for the current and prior fiscal years to support
year-over-year comparison.

## Part II — Regulatory Capital

Regulatory capital is the capital a bank must hold as required by its regulator.
The principal measures are the Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) ratio, the Tier 1
capital ratio, and the Total capital ratio.

### Item 8 — Capital Ratios (FY2024)

As of the end of fiscal year 2024, Acme Bank's Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) ratio
was 13.8%. The Tier 1 capital ratio was 15.2% and the Total capital ratio was
18.4%. All three measures were above the regulatory minimums plus applicable
buffers throughout the year.

### Item 8 — Capital Ratios (FY2023)

As of the end of fiscal year 2023, Acme Bank's Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) ratio
was 12.1%. The Tier 1 capital ratio was 13.9% and the Total capital ratio was
16.7%. The year-over-year improvement reflects retained earnings and a reduction
in risk-weighted assets.

## Part III — Capital Buffers and Surcharges

Capital buffers sit on top of the minimum capital requirements and can be drawn
down under stress.

### Capital Conservation Buffer

The capital conservation buffer is set at 2.5% of risk-weighted assets and is met
with Common Equity Tier 1 capital.

### G-SIB Surcharge

Acme Bank is designated a global systemically important bank (G-SIB). The G-SIB
surcharge applicable to Acme Bank is 1.5% of risk-weighted assets, reflecting its
bucket allocation under the assessment methodology.

## Part IV — Leverage

### Leverage Ratio

The Basel III leverage ratio was 6.3% at the end of fiscal year 2024, comfortably
above the 3.0% minimum requirement.
